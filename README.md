## Projects

### [PI Case Intake Forecasting & Growth Analytics](https://github.com/LikhithYedida/pi-intake-forecast) · [Live dashboard](https://lookerstudio.google.com/reporting/d4b3d152-2c36-40db-87b3-82bee2833d20/page/gaLAG)
Can a law firm predict when and where case demand will rise, and plan around it? Models a simulated 12-office personal injury firm on public FARS crash, weather and population data, connecting market demand, intake forecasts, staffing capacity, marketing economics and office performance. The data overturned the expected winter peak for Michigan and Ohio auto cases: the stronger peak is in summer. Validation checks and documented limitations are built into the pipeline.
`Python` `SQL` `BigQuery` `Looker Studio` `pandas` `statsmodels`

### [AI Occupational Displacement Tracker](https://github.com/LikhithYedida/job-displacement-tracker) · [Live app](https://ai-job-displacement-tracker.streamlit.app/)
Tests whether AI-exposed occupations are actually shrinking, using BLS employment data for 861 occupations (2020–2025) and two independent AI-exposure research measures. A stepwise regression shows the apparent effect disappears once sector is controlled for. Includes a 3-year forecast, a career-pivot matcher and a workforce risk simulator with PDF export.
`Python` `pandas` `statsmodels` `SciPy` `Plotly` `Streamlit` `sentence-transformers`

### [AutoPulse](https://github.com/LikhithYedida/autopulse) · [Live app](https://autopulse-3oik.onrender.com/)
Turns a VIN into an evidence-grounded vehicle safety report from four NHTSA data sources: VIN decoding, complaints, recalls and crash ratings. Includes explainable entity resolution and an AI analyst that never treats missing evidence as zero.
`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `RapidFuzz` `Anthropic API` `Render`

### [CrisisOps](https://crisisops-web-607779483839.us-central1.run.app/) · Live app
Live U.S. disaster monitoring that ranks every affected county into five priority tiers using National Weather Service alerts, population exposure, disaster history and CDC social vulnerability.
`Google Cloud Run` `NWS API` `FEMA` `CDC SVI` `Geospatial analysis`

### [Healthcare Analytics Command Center](https://github.com/LikhithYedida/healthcare-analytics-platform)
An end-to-end Azure lakehouse: Data Factory ingestion into a Bronze → Silver → Gold Databricks pipeline with built-in data-quality validation, feeding a six-page Tableau command center tracking 4,435 encounters, $14.6M in claim costs and a 15.69% 30-day readmission rate.
`Azure Data Factory` `ADLS Gen2` `Databricks` `PySpark` `Delta Lake` `Tableau`

### [Consumer Complaint Risk & Response Intelligence](https://github.com/LikhithYedida/banking-risk-intelligence)
50,000 CFPB consumer complaints ingested with Python into PostgreSQL, modeled in dbt into 7 analytics marts covered by 7 reconciliation tests, enriched with NLP topic modeling and sentiment, and reported in a four-page Power BI risk dashboard with a composite company risk score.
`Python` `PostgreSQL` `dbt` `scikit-learn` `NLP` `Power BI` `DAX`

### [Credit Card Fraud Analytics](https://github.com/LikhithYedida/creditcard_fraud_analytics)
SQL data-quality checks, Logistic Regression and Random Forest fraud models evaluated on ROC-AUC, precision and recall, a scoring script for new transactions, and a Tableau dashboard of fraud patterns.
